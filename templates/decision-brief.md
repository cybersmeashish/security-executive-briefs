# Executive decision brief template

This is an unfilled reusable template, not a client engagement.

## Decision requested

Name the bounded investment, responsible owner and approval gate.

## Business condition

Identify the critical service, affected authority/data and harm to investigate. Separate an observed incident from a plausible risk.

## Evidence

Link exact source, method, sample, result, failures and uncertainty. State what was measured and what cannot be concluded.

## Options and consequence

Compare the costed scope, retained risks and operational impact of each feasible option.

## Execution and acceptance

Name the owners, agreed review window, milestones, measurable gates and stop/rollback conditions.

## Next investment

State what additional evidence would justify expansion. Use measured financial outcomes only when verified.
